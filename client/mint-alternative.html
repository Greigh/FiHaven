When FiHaven is the wrong pick

If what you loved about Mint was automatic categorisation of every transaction across every account — investments, mortgages, the lot — FiHaven is a smaller, more deliberate tool and you'll feel the difference. It's built for people who want a clear view of bills and debt without handing over every account. If you want a full aggregator, look at Monarch Money, Copilot, or Quicken Simplifi instead. We'd rather you found the right tool than churned out of ours.
