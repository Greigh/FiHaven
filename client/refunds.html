Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how to cancel FiHaven Pro, when you can get your money back, and how to ask. It applies to the FiHaven service at fihaven.app and the official iOS and Android apps, operated by Greigh Studios LLC, a Michigan limited liability company. It sits alongside our Terms of Use.

Last updated: July 26, 2026

The short version — FiHaven is free to use, and the free tier doesn’t expire. Paid plans can be cancelled at any time in a couple of taps, and you keep access until the period you’ve already paid for runs out. If a charge caught you off guard, email support@fihaven.app within 3 days and we’ll sort it out.

1. Free tier and free trials

The manual workspace is free forever and needs no card to sign up. Where a free trial is offered — currently 7 days on the monthly and yearly Pro plans — you are not charged until the trial ends. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. The Family plan does not include a trial and bills at purchase.

Cancelling a trial takes effect immediately for billing purposes: no charge is made, and Pro features remain available until the trial period would have ended.

2. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time, and you never need to contact us to do it:

  • Bought on the web — Settings → FiHaven Pro → Manage billing, which opens the billing portal for your subscription.
  • Bought in the iOS app — Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → FiHaven.
  • Bought in the Android app — Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → FiHaven.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for, and we do not pro-rate or refund the unused part of a period except as described below. Your data is untouched by cancellation — you keep your account, and you can export everything (JSON or CSV) or delete your account entirely from Settings at any time.

3. Refunds on web purchases

Web subscriptions are sold by Paddle, our merchant of record — Paddle is the seller of record for the transaction and appears on your statement. Refund requests still come to us, and we authorize them with Paddle. For subscriptions bought on fihaven.app:

  • 3-day refund on a first purchase. If you’re within 3 days of your first paid charge and FiHaven isn’t for you, email us and we’ll refund it in full. You don’t owe us an explanation.
  • Accidental renewals. If an auto-renewal caught you out — you meant to cancel, or you hadn’t used FiHaven in that period — email us within 3 days of the charge and we’ll refund it.
  • Billing errors. Duplicate charges, a charge after a confirmed cancellation, or being billed the wrong amount are always refunded in full, however long it takes us to spot them.
  • Service failure. If a Pro feature you paid for was unavailable for a sustained period, tell us and we’ll refund or credit the affected time.
  • Otherwise, payments for a period already served are non-refundable, and part-used periods are not pro-rated.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once approved, we submit the refund immediately; how quickly it appears is up to your bank or card issuer, and usually takes 5–10 business days.

4. Purchases made in the App Store or Google Play

If you subscribed inside the iOS or Android app, Apple or Google took the payment, not us — and only they can refund it. We have no ability to issue a refund for a store purchase.

  • Apple — request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, under Apple’s own refund policy.
  • Google Play — request a refund at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions or through Google Play support, under Google’s policy.

If a store declines your request and you believe the charge was our error, email us anyway — we’ll look at what we can do, including granting Pro access directly.

5. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the law where you live. If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, you have a statutory right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days; where you asked for immediate access to a digital service and it was supplied, that right may be reduced or lost, as set out in the applicable consumer legislation. Consumers elsewhere may have similar non-waivable rights. Where local law grants you more than this policy does, the law wins.

6. Promo codes and granted access

Promo codes and complimentary Pro grants have no cash value and are not refundable or exchangeable. Cancelling a plan you obtained with a promo code does not entitle you to a payment.

7. Chargebacks

If something looks wrong on your statement, please email us before contacting your bank — we can almost always resolve it faster, and a refund reaches you sooner than a chargeback would. We may suspend an account with an unresolved chargeback until the dispute is settled.

8. How to request a refund

Email support@fihaven.app from the address on your FiHaven account, and include:

  • Whether you subscribed on the web or through Apple / Google.
  • The date and amount of the charge (a receipt or the last four digits of the card helps).
  • Anything you want us to know — optional, and never required.

We reply to refund requests within 2 business days, and typically much sooner. You can also reach us through the Contact page.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change when we do. The policy in force at the time of your purchase is the one that applies to that purchase.
