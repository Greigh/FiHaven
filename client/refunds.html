Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how to cancel FiHaven Pro, when you can get your money back, and how to ask. It applies to the FiHaven service at fihaven.app and the official iOS and Android apps, operated by Greigh Studios LLC, a Michigan limited liability company. It sits alongside our Terms of Use.

Last updated: July 29, 2026

The short version — FiHaven is free to use, and the free tier doesn’t expire. Paid plans can be cancelled at any time in a couple of taps, and you keep access until the period you’ve already paid for runs out. Every charge made on fihaven.app is refundable in full for 14 days — just email support@fihaven.app. If you subscribed inside the iOS or Android app, the refund window is Apple’s or Google’s, and it is shorter and stricter than ours — see section 4.

1. Free tier and free trials

The manual workspace is free forever and needs no card to sign up. Where a free trial is offered — currently 7 days on the monthly and yearly Pro plans — you are not charged until the trial ends. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. The Family plan does not include a trial and bills at purchase.

Cancelling a trial takes effect immediately for billing purposes: no charge is made, and Pro features remain available until the trial period would have ended.

2. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time, and you never need to contact us to do it:

  • Bought on the web — Settings → FiHaven Pro → Manage billing, which opens the billing portal for your subscription.
  • Bought in the iOS app — Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → FiHaven.
  • Bought in the Android app — Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → FiHaven.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for. If you would rather have your money back than keep access for the rest of the period, ask for a refund instead — see section 3. Your data is untouched by cancellation — you keep your account, and you can export everything (JSON or CSV) or delete your account entirely from Settings at any time.

3. Refunds on web purchases

Web subscriptions are sold by Paddle, our merchant of record — Paddle is the seller of record for the transaction and appears on your statement. Refund requests come to us, and we authorize them with Paddle. Because Paddle is the seller, your purchase is also covered by Paddle’s Buyer Terms and privacy policy. Where this policy gives you more than Paddle’s terms require, this policy applies.

You may request a refund within 14 days of any charge, and we will refund it in full. You do not need to give a reason. This applies to every charge on fihaven.app — your first purchase and every renewal after it, on the monthly, yearly, and Family plans alike.

To request one, email support@fihaven.app — see section 8 for what to include. Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once approved, we submit the refund immediately; how quickly it appears is up to your bank or card issuer, and usually takes 5–10 business days. When a refund is issued, Pro access ends and your account returns to the free tier — your data stays exactly where it is.

Beyond the 14 days, we still put billing mistakes right: duplicate charges, a charge after a confirmed cancellation, or being billed the wrong amount are refunded in full whenever we find them, however long that takes.

Sales tax and VAT. Paddle collects and remits any sales tax or VAT on web purchases. If you were charged tax you should not have paid — for example, you are a VAT-registered business or hold a valid exemption — Paddle can refund the tax portion on its own, separately from the price. Those claims must reach Paddle within 60 days of the purchase and need supporting documentation such as a valid VAT ID or exemption certificate. Email us and we will point you to the right place, or contact Paddle directly.

4. Purchases made in the App Store or Google Play

If you subscribed inside the iOS or Android app, Apple or Google took the payment, not us. That changes who decides, and — importantly — how long you have. The 14-day promise in section 3 is ours to make on fihaven.app; on the stores we can only work within the window each one sets, and the two windows are not the same.

  • Google Play — 48 hours self-service, then us. For the first 48 hours after a charge you can refund it yourself at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions; Google usually decides within minutes, though it can take up to 4 business days. After 48 hours Google stops handling it and sends you to us — so email support@fihaven.app, and we will refund the charge from the Play Console on the same 14-day terms as a web purchase. Google’s own policy is here.
  • Apple — no fixed window, and Apple’s call, not ours. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple does not publish a guaranteed refund period: App Store purchases are treated as final and each request is reviewed case by case, so a request may be granted or declined whether it is one day or one month old. In practice Report a Problem only lists purchases from roughly the last 90 days, so ask early. Decisions typically take 24–48 hours. We cannot issue, override, or appeal an App Store refund — Apple does not give developers that ability. See Apple’s refund page.

In both cases, cancelling in the store stops the next renewal but is not itself a refund — see section 2.

If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK, your 14-day right to withdraw applies to store purchases too, and both Apple and Google run separate policies to honour it. That right is against the store as seller, so exercise it through Apple or Google, not through us.

If a store declines your request and you believe the charge was our error, email us anyway — we’ll look at what we can do, including refunding it ourselves where the store allows it, or granting Pro access directly where it does not. You will never be worse off for having bought through a store because we could not act in time: if Apple refuses a refund we think you should have had, we will make it right with equivalent Pro access.

5. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the law where you live. Consumers in the EU, EEA, and UK have a statutory right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days, and consumers elsewhere may have similar non-waivable rights. The 14-day refund in section 3 is offered to everyone, wherever you are, and we do not ask you to waive anything to get it. Where local law grants you more than this policy does, the law wins.

6. Promo codes and granted access

Complimentary Pro access — granted by us, or given away in a promotion — has no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money, because there was no charge to refund. If you paid for a subscription using a discount code, that purchase is refundable on exactly the same terms as any other: within 14 days, in full, for the amount you actually paid.

7. Chargebacks

If something looks wrong on your statement, please email us before contacting your bank — we can almost always resolve it faster, and a refund reaches you sooner than a chargeback would. We may suspend an account with an unresolved chargeback until the dispute is settled.

8. How to request a refund

Email support@fihaven.app from the address on your FiHaven account, and include:

  • Whether you subscribed on the web or through Apple / Google.
  • The date and amount of the charge (a receipt or the last four digits of the card helps).
  • Anything you want us to know — optional, and never required.

We reply to refund requests within 2 business days, and typically much sooner. You can also reach us through the Contact page.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change when we do. The policy in force at the time of your purchase is the one that applies to that purchase.
