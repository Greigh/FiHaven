# Delete your FiHaven account

This page explains how to permanently delete your **FiHaven** account — operated by **Greigh Studios LLC** — and everything stored with it. You can do it yourself in a couple of taps, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Last updated: July 31, 2026

**The short version** — Sign in and go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**, on the web or in the iPhone or Android app. It is immediate and permanent. If you can’t sign in or no longer have the app, email [support@fihaven.app](mailto:support@fihaven.app) from your account’s email address and we’ll delete it for you.

### 1. Delete it yourself, in the app or on the web

The fastest route, and the one we recommend — it takes effect the moment you confirm:

- **On the web** — sign in, then go to [Settings](https://fihaven.app/settings) → Account → **Delete account**.
- **iPhone / iPad** — Settings → Account → **Delete account** (also under Settings → Data).
- **Android** — Settings → Account → **Delete account** (also under Settings → Data).

You’ll be asked to confirm by typing DELETE ACCOUNT DATA, and to re-enter your password. If you signed in with Apple or Google you don’t have a password with us, so the typed confirmation is all that’s needed. If you use two-factor authentication, you’ll also be asked for a current code.

### 2. Request deletion by email

If you’ve already uninstalled the app, can’t sign in, or would simply rather we did it, email [support@fihaven.app](mailto:support@fihaven.app?subject=Account%20deletion%20request) with the subject **Account deletion request**.

Send it **from the email address on the account** — that’s how we confirm the request is really yours. If you can’t, tell us the account email and we’ll write to that address to confirm before deleting anything. We action requests within **2 business days**, and complete deletion within **30 days** at the outside. You can also reach us through the [Contact](https://fihaven.app/contact) page.

### 3. What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes all of it. There is no deactivated or frozen state — the account record itself is deleted, and everything below goes with it:

- Your account and profile — email address, name, and password.
- Every bill, card, loan, account, and savings goal you entered.
- Your full payment and spending history, and your budgets and settings.
- All login sessions, on every device, and any passkeys or two-factor enrolment.
- **Linked banks** — any Plaid connection is revoked at Plaid as well as deleted here, so the connection stops and Plaid deletes its copy under its own retention policy.
- Your notification devices, so reminders stop immediately.
- Household sharing — if you created a household it is removed; if you had joined someone else’s, you are removed from it.

### 4. What we keep, and for how long

Two narrow exceptions, both required of us rather than chosen by us:

- **Billing and tax records** — if you ever paid for FiHaven Pro, the record of that transaction is retained for as long as tax and accounting law requires. It is held by the payment provider (Paddle, Apple, or Google) and contains none of the financial data you entered into the app.
- **Email suppression** — if you unsubscribed from a mailing, we keep the fact that your address opted out, so that a future signup doesn’t start emailing you again.

Our server backups are **weekly snapshots kept for about two weeks**, held only for disaster recovery — we never use them to restore an individual account, so deleting is not undone by a restore. A snapshot taken before you deleted still holds that data until it is superseded, which is why the outside figure is about 14 days rather than instant. See our [Privacy Policy](https://fihaven.app/privacy) for the full picture.

### 5. Before you delete: two things worth doing

- **Export your data first.** Settings → Data → **Export data** downloads everything as JSON (and CSV for bills, cards, and history). Once the account is gone we cannot recover any of it — we have no way to restore a deleted account, and neither do you.
- **Cancel an App Store or Play subscription yourself.** If you subscribed inside the iPhone or Android app, Apple or Google holds that subscription and only you can cancel it — deleting your FiHaven account does not, because we have no way to reach it. Cancel in **Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions** on iPhone or **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions** on Android *before* you delete. A subscription bought on **fihaven.app** is different: we cancel that one for you as part of deleting the account, so it stops billing straight away. If you are ever charged after deleting, email [support@fihaven.app](mailto:support@fihaven.app) and we will refund it. See [Refunds & Cancellation](https://fihaven.app/refunds).

### 6. Questions

Email [support@fihaven.app](mailto:support@fihaven.app) or use the [Contact](https://fihaven.app/contact) page. If you want to remove specific data without deleting your whole account, Settings → Data → **Clear data** erases bills, cards, payment history, or bank links individually and leaves the account intact.
