# Frequently asked questions

FiHaven is a focused bill and debt dashboard — not a bank, not an advisor, not an ad network. These are the questions people usually ask before they trust it with their month.

Still stuck? See [Contact](https://fihaven.app/contact) for where to send your note.

### Getting started

What is FiHaven, exactly?

A calm workspace for recurring bills, credit cards, loans, spending, and payoff planning. You enter what you owe and when it’s due; FiHaven shows what’s left this month, what’s coming up, and how extra payments would move your debt.

It’s live on the **web**, on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fihaven/id6781084347) for **iPhone / iPad**, and on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.fihaven) for **Android**. Same account, same data, all three.

Do I need to link my bank?

No. FiHaven is designed to work fully by hand. Bank linking through Plaid is an **optional Pro feature** if you want balances and transactions imported alongside what you typed in.

Imported transactions never overwrite your manual entries — they sit next to them.

Is FiHaven a bank or financial advisor?

No. FiHaven doesn’t hold your money, move funds, or give personalized financial advice. It’s software you use to organize information you already have.

### Free vs Pro

What’s included for free?

Unlimited bills, cards, and loans; dashboard; budget and rolling pay periods; manual spending log; savings goals; net worth; income sources; mark-paid / partial payments / skip-this-month; JSON and CSV export; and full account deletion.

See the full comparison on [Pricing](https://fihaven.app/pricing).

What does FiHaven Pro add?

- Payoff planner (snowball, avalanche, minimums)
- Calendar view and private iCal feed
- Full payment history
- Rewards optimizer for credit cards
- Subscription finder (recurring charges and price hikes)
- Per-category budgets
- Optional Plaid bank linking
- Autopay auto-mark on due dates

How much is Pro, and is there a trial?

Pro is **$1.99/month** everywhere. Yearly is **$14.99 on the web** and **$16.99 on iOS/Android**. The **Family** plan (create a household of up to three) is **$25.99/yr on the web** and **$29.99/yr on the stores**. Joining someone else’s household with an invite is free.

The monthly and yearly Pro plans include a **7-day free trial**; **Family has no trial** and bills right away. You can **cancel anytime** — on the web through the billing portal, or in your Apple / Google subscription settings. See [Pricing](https://fihaven.app/pricing) for the full breakdown.

How does Family sharing work?

**Family** lets you create a household of **up to three people** and share bills, cards, and savings goals across it. Everyone keeps their own login.

- **Joining is free.** Members accept an invite and need no subscription of their own — only the person who creates the household pays.
- **Free and Pro can’t create one.** Creating a household is the Family plan’s job; Free and Pro accounts can join someone else’s.
- **If the plan lapses, nothing is deleted.** The shared household goes read-only — everyone can still see it, but it stops accepting changes until the plan is renewed.

How do I upgrade or cancel Pro?

On the web, open **FiHaven Pro** from the account menu after you sign in — checkout is handled by Paddle, our merchant of record, and includes the 7-day trial on monthly and yearly. Manage or cancel from the customer portal there.

In the iOS or Android apps, subscribe through Apple or Google — monthly and yearly Pro carry the same 7-day intro trial there. Manage or cancel in your store subscription settings. Pro follows your account across platforms, and each app tells you where your particular subscription is managed, so a plan bought on one platform is never a dead end on another.

Promo codes, when offered, can be redeemed in the same Pro dialog.

### How features behave

How do bill schedules work?

Bills aren’t limited to monthly. You can set weekly, bi-weekly, quarterly, or annual schedules, plus optional start and end dates for things that don’t run forever. Due dates flow through the dashboard, upcoming list, budget obligations, calendar (Pro), and autopay (Pro).

What does autopay auto-mark do?

If you turn it on for a bill or card (Pro), FiHaven marks the item paid on its due date when you’ve indicated it’s on autopay. It’s a convenience layer — you can still edit or undo payment history.

What is the subscription finder?

A Pro tool that surfaces recurring charges you might have forgotten — from bills you flagged as subscriptions and from spending patterns (same merchant across multiple months). It also calls out price increases when it spots them.

What is the rewards optimizer?

FiHaven ships a built-in database of common card reward categories. Pro suggests which card to use for a purchase category based on the cards you’ve added. Adding or editing a card can auto-suggest a preset from that database.

Can I track 0% promo APR deadlines?

Yes. Enter promo balance, rate, and end date on a card. The dashboard highlights cards whose 0% window is ending and the payoff planner (Pro) can factor promo minimums into strategies.

How do bill reminders reach me?

Two independent routes, both optional and both configured in Settings → Notifications:

- **Email** — reminders ahead of a due date, plus a monthly summary and weekly digest if you want them. Every one carries a one-click unsubscribe.
- **Push / on-device** — the iPhone and Android apps can schedule reminders on the device itself, so they arrive whether or not the app is open. You can pick more than one lead time (say, three days before and again on the day).

Device reminders belong to the signed-in session: **signing out clears them**, and they come back when you sign in again.

Does my data sync between the web and my phone?

Yes. There’s one account and one copy of your data behind the web app, iPhone, iPad, and Android — add a bill on your phone and it’s on your laptop, and your Pro entitlement is worked out on the server so it applies everywhere no matter where you subscribed.

Does FiHaven work offline?

Yes, on all three. FiHaven keeps a copy of your data on the device you’re using, so it opens and works without a connection.

- **You can still read everything** — bills, cards, balances, history — exactly as you left it.
- **You can still make changes.** They’re written to your device first, before anything is sent, and marked as not-yet-synced.
- **Closing the app is safe.** An unsent change survives the app being closed, force-quit, or the browser being shut down, and is sent the next time you open FiHaven or your connection comes back.

You’ll see an **Offline** marker while that’s the case, and it clears once your changes have reached your account. One caveat: because a change is synced as a whole snapshot, editing the *same* account offline on two devices at once means the one that reconnects last wins.

Can I search my bills, cards, and spending?

Yes. Bills, Cards / Loans, Subscriptions, and Spending each have a search field on web, iPhone, and Android so long lists are easier to scan.

What happens when I archive a bill or card?

Archiving retires an item without destroying it — the payment history stays, but the item stops taking part in your month. Once archived, a bill or card:

- disappears from your lists, dashboard, and upcoming view;
- stops counting toward your monthly bill total, card debt, and net worth;
- stops sending reminders — on your phone, by email, and by push;
- stops being auto-marked paid if it was on autopay.

Onboarding and Settings can prefer **archive instead of delete** (on by default once you finish setup), so retiring something is the normal path and deleting is the exception. You can unarchive at any time.

### Your data & account

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No ads, no data brokerage. FiHaven makes money from optional Pro subscriptions — not from selling your financial picture. See [Privacy](https://fihaven.app/privacy) for the full picture.

Can I export or delete everything?

Yes. Export JSON or CSV from Settings anytime. Account deletion removes your user record and associated data from our servers. Disconnecting Plaid deletes the access token and linked account data.

What sign-in security is available?

Passwords are bcrypt-hashed. Sessions use secure HttpOnly cookies on the web. You can sign in with Apple or Google, add an authenticator app (TOTP), email codes, or a passkey. If MFA is enabled, it still runs after social sign-in. Sign-up and sign-in are rate-limited and protected by Cloudflare Turnstile. More detail on [Security](https://fihaven.app/security).

Is FiHaven open source?

The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Greigh/FiHaven) for transparency, but FiHaven is **source available**, not open source — you can read and contribute, but you may not run a public hosted copy or redistribute modified builds without permission. It’s published by Greigh Studios LLC under the [Greigh Studios Source Available License](https://github.com/Greigh/FiHaven/blob/main/LICENSE).
