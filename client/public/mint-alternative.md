# Mint is gone. Your bills still aren't.

Intuit shut Mint down and pointed everyone at Credit Karma — a credit and offers product, not a budgeting tool. FiHaven is built for the part most people actually miss: seeing what's due, what's owed, and what the month looks like, in one calm place.

## How FiHaven differs

|  | Mint (discontinued) | FiHaven |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank linking | Required — the app was built around aggregation | Optional. Everything works by hand; linking is a Pro extra |
| Business model | Free, funded by ads and partner offers | No ads, no data brokerage — funded by optional subscriptions |
| Focus | Broad aggregator across every account type | Bills, cards, and loans, with budgeting and payoff planning |
| Debt payoff | Limited | Snowball, avalanche, and minimum-payment planning (Pro) |
| Offline use | No | Yes, on web, iPhone, iPad, and Android |
| Getting your data out | Ended with the shutdown | JSON and CSV export at any time |
| Cost | Free | Free forever by hand · Pro from $1.99/mo |

### When FiHaven is the wrong pick

If what you loved about Mint was automatic categorisation of every transaction across every account — investments, mortgages, the lot — FiHaven is a smaller, more deliberate tool and you'll feel the difference. It's built for people who want a clear view of bills and debt without handing over every account. If you want a full aggregator, look at Monarch Money, Copilot, or Quicken Simplifi instead. We'd rather you found the right tool than churned out of ours.

## What you get free

No credit card, no trial clock, no "free for 30 days then we ask."

### Bills on any schedule

Weekly through annual, with active date windows, partial payments, and skip-this-month for the ones that don't show up every cycle.

### Cards & loans

Balances, rates, and 0% promo windows in one place, so you can see what's actually costing you money.

### Budget & runway

Income sources, rolling pay periods, a manual spending log, savings goals, and net worth.

### Everywhere, offline

Web, iPhone, iPad, and Android on one account. Each keeps a local copy, so the app opens and works without a connection.

### Your data stays yours

Export to JSON or CSV whenever you like, and delete your account permanently whenever you're done.

### No ads, ever

FiHaven doesn't sell data or run partner offers. The only revenue is optional Pro and Family subscriptions.
