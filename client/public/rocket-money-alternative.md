# A bill tracker that doesn't need your bank.

Rocket Money's best tricks — spotting subscriptions, negotiating bills — only work once it can read your transactions. FiHaven starts from the opposite end: type in what you owe, see your month clearly, and connect an account later only if you want to.

## Two different bargains

|  | Rocket Money | FiHaven |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank linking | Central — most features depend on it | Optional. Manual entry is the default path |
| Bill negotiation | Yes, for a share of what it saves you | Not offered — no percentage of your savings, ever |
| Subscription handling | Finds and cancels them for you | Finds them and shows you; you cancel (Pro) |
| Debt payoff planning | Limited | Snowball, avalanche, and minimums (Pro) |
| Card perks & rewards | Not a focus | Rewards optimizer, perks tracker, and offers (Pro) |
| Offline use | No | Yes, on every platform |

| Pricing shape | Free tier plus premium, and a cut of negotiated savings | Free forever by hand · flat $1.99/mo Pro · no cuts |

### When Rocket Money is the better call

If you genuinely want someone else to phone your cable company and argue the bill down, Rocket Money does that and FiHaven does not. Same for hands-off subscription cancellation — their concierge model is a real service, and paying a share of the savings can be worth it. FiHaven is for people who'd rather hold the steering wheel: no account connection required, no percentage taken, and a clearer picture of debt payoff and card rewards than a negotiation service is built to give.

## What you actually get

### Manual-first, always

Every feature works on data you typed in. Link a bank if it helps; nothing breaks if you never do.

### Payoff planner

Snowball, avalanche, or minimums — see what each strategy does to your timeline and interest.

### Rewards optimizer

Which card to use where, plus a perks tracker so annual-fee benefits don't quietly go unused.

### Calendar & iCal feed

Due dates as a calendar, plus a private feed you can subscribe to from your own calendar app.

### Family household

Share bills, cards, and savings goals with up to two other people. They join free, on their own accounts.

### Flat pricing

$1.99 a month, or free forever if you're happy doing it by hand. No cut of your savings.
