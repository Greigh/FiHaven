Contact

# Get the right human (it’s usually just me)

FiHaven is built and operated by **Greigh Studios LLC** — a one-person studio run by [Daniel Hipskind](https://danielhipskind.com), not a support farm. Sending your note to the right place means a faster, useful reply.

**Try in the app first** — many answers live in [FAQ](https://fihaven.app/faq) or Settings: export your data (JSON/CSV), disconnect Plaid, manage Pro billing (customer portal), change password/MFA, or delete your account entirely. No email required for any of that.

### Where to send what

#### Product & billing help

Confused by a feature, stuck on onboarding, promo code didn’t work, or a charge looked wrong.

[support@fihaven.app](mailto:support@fihaven.app)

#### Getting the apps

FiHaven is on [the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fihaven/id6781084347) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.fihaven). Trouble installing or signing in?

[daniel@fihaven.app](mailto:daniel@fihaven.app)

#### Privacy & your data

Privacy questions, export/deletion help, or exercising access rights under GDPR/CCPA.

[security@fihaven.app](mailto:security@fihaven.app)

#### Security issues

Suspected vulnerabilities or account compromise. Please don’t post these publicly.

[Private GitHub advisory](https://github.com/Greigh/FiHaven/security/advisories/new)
[security@fihaven.app](mailto:security@fihaven.app)

#### Bugs & feature ideas

Reproducible bugs or thoughtful feature requests — especially if you can point at the screen or attach steps.

[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Greigh/FiHaven/issues)

### What to include

- The email on your FiHaven account (never your password).
- Browser or device, and whether you’re on web or testing a native build.
- For billing: whether you subscribed on the web (Paddle) or through Apple / Google on a native build.
- For bugs: what you expected, what happened, and the smallest steps to reproduce.

### Response time

There’s no 24/7 desk — this is a small product with real users, not enterprise SLA territory. Security reports get priority. Most other mail gets a reply within a few business days. If your account is locked out, say so in the subject line.

### Mail you’ll get from us

Transactional email only: verification, password reset, security codes, due-date reminders if you opt in, and occasional account notices. From no-reply@fihaven.app; replies to support mail route to [support@fihaven.app](mailto:support@fihaven.app). We don’t send marketing newsletters.
