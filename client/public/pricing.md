### How Pro billing works

- **7-day free trial** on the monthly and yearly Pro plans. Card or store account required to start; cancel before the trial ends and you won’t be charged. **Family doesn’t include a trial** — it bills right away.
- **Web:** checkout by Paddle, our merchant of record, after you sign in (account menu → FiHaven Pro). Pro is **$1.99/mo** or **$14.99/yr**; Family is **$25.99/yr**. The exact total is always shown at checkout before you pay.
- **iPhone, iPad & Android:** subscribe in-app through Apple or Google — **$1.99/mo**, **$16.99/yr**, or Family **$29.99/yr**. Entitlement is server-side, so Pro follows your account across every platform you sign in on.
- **Cancel anytime:** the Paddle customer portal on the web, or your Apple / Google subscription settings on native. Access continues through the period you’ve paid for.
- **Refunds:** every web charge is refundable in full within **14 days**, no questions asked. Store purchases run on the store’s clock instead: **Google Play is self-service for 48 hours** and after that comes to us, while **Apple sets no fixed window** and decides each request itself. Full detail in the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](https://fihaven.app/refunds).
- **Promo codes:** occasionally offered; redeem in the Pro dialog. Terms are stated with each code.
- **Plaid is optional:** FiHaven works fully without bank linking. Pro automation layers on top without overwriting manual entries.

Billing questions? See [Contact](https://fihaven.app/contact). Legal terms: [Terms of Use](https://fihaven.app/terms) section 5 and the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](https://fihaven.app/refunds).
