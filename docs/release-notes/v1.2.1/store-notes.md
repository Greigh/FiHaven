## App Store — What's New in This Version

```
BILLS & SPENDING

Weekly, bi-weekly, quarterly and annual bills, with real due dates rather than a
monthly approximation. Spending gets its own tab, separate from Budget.
Subscriptions screens arrive on iOS and Android.

NATIVE APPS

FiHaven branding and app icons, cleaner bill and card interactions, and the
ability to edit or delete a payment history entry with a long-press.

SETTINGS

Hide fully paid items on the dashboard. Set a bio-lock grace period, so Face ID
or your fingerprint isn't demanded again the moment you switch apps.
```
