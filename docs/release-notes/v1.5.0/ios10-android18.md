## App Store — What's New in This Version

```
THE LAUNCH-CANDIDATE BUILD

NET WORTH

Assets minus debts, with your savings, checking, investment and property
accounts, on web, iOS and Android. It's free, not a Pro feature, and no longer
buried on the Cards tab.

ARCHIVE INSTEAD OF DELETE

Retire a bill, card or loan without losing its history. Archived items drop out
of due dates, totals, the calendar and reminders, and can be restored any time.
Turn it on in Settings; each tab has a "Show archived" filter.

NOTIFICATIONS

Push notifications on iOS and Android for bill reminders, weekly digests and
monthly summaries, alongside email and on-device reminders. Browser
notifications too, from web Settings, no app needed. Notification settings now
split into On this device, Email, and Reminder timing.

BUDGET & SPENDING

Budget lenses (50/30/20, envelopes, debt-focus and more) are now in Settings on
iOS and Android, not just the web. Envelope editor and spending insights for
Pro. Households see a shared dashboard card with combined upcoming bills and
balances.

SUBSCRIPTIONS

Recognized services show their real logo. Jump straight to the bill behind a
subscription, and save a manage or cancel link for it. Note: offering a link to
share with us sends the service name, the link and your email address — that's
now spelled out in the app and in the Privacy Policy. Saving a link only to your
own bill sends us nothing.

CARDS

Payoff plan for 0% cards: what to pay in a lump sum, alongside the monthly
amount needed to clear each 0%-financing card before its promo ends. Set a
separate autopay day from the due date. Recurring card perks and activated
offers get tracked before they expire.

MONTHLY ROLLOVER

When a new month starts, FiHaven offers to review each bill's amount, and names
anything from last month that was never marked paid. A quarterly bill now says
"Paid this quarter" rather than always "this month".

BANK LINKING

Live in production on fihaven.app, not dev-only. If your bank adds accounts
later, link them without starting over.
```
