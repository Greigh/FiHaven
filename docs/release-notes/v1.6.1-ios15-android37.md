Paste-ready copy for the store consoles. Neither upload script reads these —
`scripts/play-upload.js` and `scripts/ios-testflight.sh` push the binary only, so

## Google Play — What's new (en-US)

> 473 / 500 characters (counted with newlines, as the console does).

```
History now charts income against spending, so you can see what you actually
keep each month. Card payments no longer count as spending — they settle
purchases already counted, which was doubling the figure. Months with nothing
logged stay blank instead of showing $0.

Cards now lead with the amount you pick — due, current, or left to pay — and
show their bank's real logo.

Utilization follows your current balance, so a card used since its statement
no longer reads 0%.
```

## TestFlight — What to Test

> 2096 / 4000 characters.

```
WHAT'S NEW IN BUILD 15

Income vs. spending in History
The income panel used to be one bar per month, scaled against your biggest
month. Income barely moves, so every bar ran nearly full width and they all
looked identical. It's a two-line chart now: income and spending, oldest month
on the left, with the gap between them shaded. That gap is your net.

There's a new headline number — average net per month — with average income and
average spending beside it.

Card payments no longer count as spending. Paying your Chase bill isn't money
leaving your pocket; the purchases already were, when you made them. Counting
both was doubling your card spending.

Months we can't account for are left blank rather than drawn as $0. If you made
card payments but never logged what you bought, the line breaks and the month is
shaded — showing $0 there would have read as a great month. Those months also
stay out of your averages.

Cards
Pick which of a card's three amounts gets the big figure: amount due, current
balance, or what's left to pay this period. What's due moved to the top-right
corner of every card.

Utilization now measures against your current balance, not the statement — a
card that closed at zero but has been used since no longer reads 0%.

Cards show their bank's real logo instead of a colored dot, with 26 more added.
Issuers we can't bundle a logo for get their initials on a brand-colored chip.

WHAT TO TEST

1. History tab. Does the chart match what you'd expect from your own months? The
   table underneath has the exact figures — check a month or two against it.
2. If you have credit cards, confirm your spending line looks right and isn't
   inflated by card payments.
3. If any month reads "not recorded", that's months where card payments happened
   but no purchases were logged. Confirm that matches your data.
4. Cards tab. Try each headline option in Settings > Cards and check the corner
   amount and utilization look right.
5. Dark mode on both screens.

KNOWN

The chart hasn't had a full pass on small screens yet — flag any clipping or
overlapping labels.
```
