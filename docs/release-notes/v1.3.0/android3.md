## App Store — What's New in This Version

```
DASHBOARD

Switch between the classic layout and reorderable widgets — overview, cash flow,

REMINDERS

Bill reminders by email, and optional notifications on your phone. Choose how

Branded Sign in with Apple and Google buttons on web, iOS and Android.

INCOME

An income history chart, and support for hourly pay — enter your rate and hours
per week.
```
